# Rosscore Labs — South African Legal & Compliance Checklist

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Confirm the actual legal structure and get South African professional advice where the situation requires it. Do not claim Rosscore Labs is a registered company until its CIPC status and registered legal name have been verified.

## 1. Business identity and contracting
- Verify whether Rosscore Labs is a registered company, a registered business name, or a trading/brand name used by an individual or another entity.
- Use the correct contracting party's full legal name and status on quotes, invoices, service terms and any website disclosures.
- If the supplier is a juristic person, verify the registration number and official details before publishing them.
- Provide customers with a written quote/order that defines deliverables, exclusions, milestones, deposit, balance due, delivery dependencies, revision limits, cancellation/refund handling, support period and change-request rates.
- Clarify domain registration ownership, hosting account ownership, renewal costs, access credentials, backup responsibility and what happens if the client stops paying for third-party services.
- Do not promise rankings, leads, revenue, security, uptime or delivery dates beyond what the agreed scope and dependencies support.
- Keep invoices, payment records, client approvals and handover records.

## 2. Consumer and e-commerce disclosures
Where the service is offered or contracted electronically, review applicable requirements under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA), Consumer Protection Act (CPA), and other applicable laws. Before taking an online order or payment, have the appropriate supplier identity, contact details, service description, total price and additional costs, payment method, terms, and cancellation/refund information available as required for the transaction. Applicability and exceptions depend on the exact transaction; do not treat this checklist as a legal determination.

## 3. POPIA and personal information
- Decide what personal information Rosscore collects, why it is needed, who can access it, where it is stored, and when it is deleted.
- Collect only information needed for enquiries and delivery; restrict access and use reasonable safeguards.
- Add a privacy notice explaining the responsible party, purpose, lawful basis/justification, recipients, retention, security/contact process and data-subject rights as applicable.
- If forms collect personal information, ensure the form and backend actually work and route submissions securely; a mailto link is not a server-side lead system.
- If Rosscore processes customer data on a client's behalf, document roles, instructions, safeguards, breach handling, retention/deletion and any sub-processors in the service agreement.
- Do not add analytics, tracking pixels, mailing lists or third-party embeds without checking their data implications and required disclosures/permissions.
- Assess whether the Information Officer must be registered and whether PAIA documentation or other formal obligations apply to the actual entity and processing activities.

## 4. Copyright, imagery and client permissions
- Use original work, client-authorised assets, or assets with a verified license permitting the intended commercial use.
- Keep a source URL, creator, license, download date and any attribution requirement for each third-party image, font, icon, video and template.
- Stock photography must be clearly illustrative; do not imply stock subjects are the client's employees, customers, premises or vehicles.
- Do not copy competitor text, logos, photos, reviews or distinctive branding.
- Obtain written permission before using a client's name, logo, screenshots, testimonials, analytics or website as a public case study.
- Confirm the client's authority to supply and authorise use of all materials they provide.
- Use written terms to define ownership/licensing of custom deliverables, reusable components, third-party assets and source files.

## 5. Marketing and outreach
- Make claims truthful, substantiated and current. No invented reviews, ratings, client counts, credentials or guarantees.
- Keep outreach targeted and relevant; respect opt-outs and applicable rules for unsolicited electronic marketing and personal information.
- Do not use automated bulk messaging or harvested personal contact data without a proper compliance review.
- Identify Rosscore accurately and provide a clear way for prospects to decline further contact.

## 6. Brand and intellectual property
- Search CIPC records for potentially conflicting company names and trade marks before significant investment in the brand.
- A domain or social handle does not itself establish trade-mark rights.
- Use the verified contracting entity's legal identity in contracts even if Rosscore Labs is the public-facing brand.

## 7. Before first paid project
- [ ] Confirm the legal contracting party and its details.
- [ ] Finalise quote, scope, service terms and invoice template.
- [ ] Define deposit and refund/cancellation handling.
- [ ] Publish appropriate supplier/contact disclosures on Rosscore's own site.
- [ ] Publish a privacy notice appropriate to actual data collection.
- [ ] Confirm Information Officer / PAIA requirements with official guidance.
- [ ] Create an asset provenance register and client authorisation checklist.
- [ ] Create a launch sign-off and handover checklist.
- [ ] Confirm tax, invoicing and recordkeeping requirements with SARS/accounting guidance.
