# Rosscore Labs — Website Sales Launch

## Objective
Win the first paying website customer. Do not build more demo sites until outreach produces evidence of demand.

## Two lead offers (proposed prices; confirm scope in writing)
- **Business Starter — R1,500:** one-page mobile-responsive website, services summary, contact details and one direct enquiry path, basic page title/description, one consolidated revision. Target: 3 business days after deposit and receipt of approved content/assets.
- **Business Website — R3,500:** up to five pages, responsive layout, clear call/WhatsApp enquiry path and basic on-page SEO, one consolidated revision. Target: 5–7 business days after deposit and receipt of approved content/assets.

Quote domain, hosting, email, paid services, copywriting, photography, extra revisions and maintenance separately unless explicitly included. Do not promise unverified features or outcomes.

## Prospecting
1. Find ten local businesses with no website, a broken site, unclear services or difficult mobile contact.
2. Record the business, public contact source, one specific issue, outreach date, response and next action.
3. Send individual, relevant messages; respect opt-outs and applicable electronic-marketing/privacy rules.
4. Show the closest demo, then send a written quote and scope.
5. Collect an agreed deposit before starting.
6. Obtain written approval for content, images, claims and publication.
7. Test mobile/desktop layouts and every contact link; obtain client sign-off before handover.

## Outreach template
Hi, I'm reaching out from Rosscore Labs. I help small businesses make it easier for customers to understand their services and contact them from a phone. I noticed [specific observation] and have a relevant example I can show you. A one-page business website starts at a proposed R1,500, subject to confirmed scope. Would you like to see the example?

## Non-negotiable gates
- Written scope, price, payment milestones, exclusions and revision limits.
- Client authority for business name, logo, contact details, claims and supplied assets.
- Image source/license record.
- Accurate contact details and working enquiry actions.
- Appropriate privacy notice if personal information is collected.
- Client approval before publication.
- No fabricated reviews, ratings, credentials, guarantees, locations or performance claims.
- Keep quote, invoice, approvals and handover records.

## First milestone
One qualified conversation and one paid deposit. Portfolio polishing is secondary to outreach and follow-up.
